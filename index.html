Materials legend

Underground lift guides: 48 mirrored iron-chain guides, five blocks tall (world Y 121–125), hang beneath the spruce-plank ceiling at Y 126, never beneath trapdoors. Ten mirrored chain pairs hang along the two OUTER edges of the central wooden passage at Z 493 and Z 496, leaving its middle open; the other 28 chain guides retain mirrored upper/lower and left/right groups. Sixteen hanging lanterns are inboard, at Z 494 and Z 495 (Y 125), between the two outer chain lines. Chains are a Minecraft substitute for historical lift ropes.

This is a Minecraft-friendly reconstruction blueprint, not a verified archaeological block-by-block survey. Nothing in this HTML requires a connection or a mod.
